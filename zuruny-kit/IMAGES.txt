==============================================================================
ZURUNY — IMAGE MANIFEST
What every file is, and what it is good for.
==============================================================================

All product photography is dark, studio-lit still life on black or oxblood
grounds. Dimensions are given because aspect ratio decides where a shot can
be used without cropping through the middle of the object.


==============================================================================
BRAND ASSETS  —  images/brand/
==============================================================================

emblem.png              1272 x 1264
  The quatrefoil mark with the olive tree inside, as a white silhouette on
  transparency. Intended to be used as a CSS mask painted with currentColor,
  so it can be any brand colour at any size. This is the real mark lifted
  from the brand deck, not a redrawing.

emblem-mark.png         1272 x 1264
  The same mark pre-filled in oxblood (#51110F). Use where a plain <img> is
  needed and tinting is not possible.

logo-lockup.png         1272 x 1584
  Emblem above the ZURUNY wordmark. The full logo. Same white-on-transparent
  treatment, so tint it.

pattern-sprig.png       265 x 154
  Small repeating leaf sprig from the deck. TILES SEAMLESSLY — verified.
  Good as a whole-page texture at very low opacity (~4%). At full strength
  it is far too busy.

pattern-damask.png      1052 x 665
  Large damask scroll from the deck. Rich and handsome, but the repeat is
  not clean enough for an endless background. Use it inside one contained
  block — a dark footer panel, a single band — not as page wallpaper.

em-ramiz.mp4            2.2 MB, 1280x720, 8 seconds, has audio
em-ramiz.webm           1.7 MB, same film, better compression
em-ramiz-poster.jpg     first frame, for the poster attribute

  A slow push-in from three Em Ramiz tins to a tight shot of the gold
  emblem. It has a real arc and it ends on the mark.

  If you loop it, dip to black across the seam — cutting straight from the
  tight logo back to the wide shot snaps hard. Serve both files, mute it for
  autoplay, and pause it when it scrolls out of view.
